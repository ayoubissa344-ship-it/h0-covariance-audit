Dear Editor,

Please consider the attached manuscript, “Provenance and covariance sensitivity in a summary-level comparison of sound-horizon-free Hubble-constant measurements,” as a research note on reproducibility and covariance assumptions in published H0 compilations.

The manuscript provides a scoped audit of a recently published 88-entry compilation. It reproduces the summary-level inverse-variance and illustrative GLS calculations, records a source/version traceability mismatch for one high-weight entry, and quantifies the diagonal inverse-variance weight associated with three DESI DR2-input estimates. It explicitly distinguishes these diagnostics from a joint-likelihood reanalysis and does not claim that the Hubble tension is resolved or that the source paper is invalid.

We believe the manuscript may be relevant to readers concerned with reproducible cosmological inference, dependent estimates, and the interpretation of summary-level significance. The analysis scripts and CSV diagnostics are included with the submission files.

This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. The author declares no competing interests and received no dedicated funding for this work.
